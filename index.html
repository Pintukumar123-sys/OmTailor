Our story

Crafting Elegance, Stitch by Stitch.

OM TAILOR is a boutique tailoring studio in Nagwa, Birgunj, built around one idea: clothes should be made for the person who wears them. Every garment starts with a conversation and a careful measurement, and moves through cutting, stitching and finishing with attention at each step.

From everyday wear to wedding outfits, we help you choose the right fabric, cut and detailing — and our customer portal lets you follow your order until it is ready.

  • Personal measurements saved in a digital measurement locker
  • Fabric guidance — use your own cloth or choose from the shop
  • Stage-by-stage order tracking, from measurement to delivery
